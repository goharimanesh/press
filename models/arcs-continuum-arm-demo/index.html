Model. Each section of length L = 60 mm bends with constant curvature κi, so its end is x = (sin(φ+θ) − sin φ)/κ, y = (cos φ − cos(φ+θ))/κ with θ = κL. Inverse kinematics solves Δθ = Jᵀ(JJᵀ + λ²I)⁻¹ e every frame (limits ±150° per section). Tendons sit r = 8 mm from the backbone, so a tendon ending at section i shortens by Δl = r·Σθj≤i.
